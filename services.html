Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How do I pair Cozy Light with my phone?

+

Turn on Cozy Light and enable Bluetooth on your phone. Go to your Bluetooth settings and look for "Cozy Light Pro" in the available devices list. Tap to connect. The LED will flash blue when in pairing mode and stay solid blue when connected.

How long does the battery last?

+

The Cozy Light Pro has a 2000mAh battery that lasts 6-8 hours depending on usage. With the light on full brightness and mist at maximum, expect around 4-5 hours. With just the light on low, you can get up to 8 hours.

Can I use essential oils with Cozy Light?

+

We do not recommend adding essential oils to Cozy Light as they may damage the ultrasonic mechanism and void the warranty. Cozy Light is designed to work with clean water only. If you want aromatherapy, consider our Cozy Light Aroma model (coming soon).

How do I clean my Cozy Light?

+

Unplug the device and empty the water tank. Wipe the exterior with a soft, damp cloth. For the water tank, rinse with clean water weekly. For deep cleaning, use a mixture of white vinegar and water (1:1 ratio) once a month. Never submerge the device or use harsh chemicals.

What is the coverage area for the humidifier?

+

Cozy Light Pro is designed for personal spaces up to 200 square feet (18 square meters). It works best in bedrooms, offices, and small living rooms. For larger spaces, consider placing multiple units or using our Cozy Light Max model.

How do I claim warranty?

+

To claim warranty, email support@cozylight.com with your order number, product serial number (found on the bottom of the device), and a description of the issue. Photos or videos of the problem are helpful. Our team will respond within 24 hours and guide you through the process.

Do you ship internationally?

+

Yes! We ship to over 30 countries worldwide. Shipping times vary by location: 5-7 business days within the US, 7-14 days to Europe, and 10-20 days to other regions. Free shipping is available for orders over $25 in the US.

Can I use Cozy Light while charging?

+

Yes, Cozy Light can be used while charging via USB-C. However, the battery will charge more slowly when the device is in use. For fastest charging, turn off the device while charging.
